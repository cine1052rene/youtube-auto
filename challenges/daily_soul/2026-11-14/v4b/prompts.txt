Cinematic photorealistic still life, shadows have their own plans. A quiet old Mediterranean alley in strong late-afternoon sun. On the pale stone ground, right in front of a plain whitewashed plaster wall, stands one small terracotta flower pot holding a single tall sunflower in full bloom, its golden face lifted toward the sun, petals glowing. One low, strong sun from behind and slightly to the side of the pot casts the pot's single long shadow: the shadow starts exactly at the base of the terracotta pot, joined to it with no gap, runs across the stone ground and climbs up onto the white wall. On the wall, the shadow is NOT the shape of a flower: it is the clear, crisp silhouette of a person kneeling on the ground with head bowed and both hands pressed together in prayer, facing toward the sun, as if the sunflower's shadow is quietly praying to the sun. The real sunflower is just a normal flower; only its shadow has become a praying person. Camera: eye-level shot from slightly to the side, 50mm lens, deep focus, the small pot and sunflower in the lower left, the continuous shadow running from the pot base across the ground and up the wall, the praying silhouette large and clearly readable on the wall. Lighting: warm low golden sunlight, long sharp-edged shadow, soft warm bounce light on the white wall, cool blue-grey shadow tone. Color palette: white plaster, golden yellow petals, terracotta orange, warm stone beige, cool blue-grey shadow, fine film grain.
