A cinematic photograph taken from just behind a person standing in an ordinary cozy apartment hallway: their hand on the brass doorknob has just pulled open a plain white wooden closet door, winter coats hanging on wall hooks beside it. Through the open doorway, instead of a closet, lies a whole prehistoric stone-age world made entirely of needle-felted wool: soft felted ferns and moss, a gently smoking felt volcano on the horizon, felt boulders, a tiny felted campfire with a stone axe beside it. Just inside the doorway, peeking out from behind a big mossy felt boulder, is a small cream-white needle-felted bear with round ears and pink blush cheeks wearing a tiny leopard-print caveman tunic, and on top of the boulder stands a tiny round yellow felted chick on both feet, only one third the width of the bear's head, about the size of a walnut. Both lock eyes with the person opening the door and freeze in surprise: the bear's eyes go wide with its mouth in a small round o and no teeth visible, the chick's feathers puff up. Behind the boulder, a big friendly cream-white felted dinosaur with soft rounded back spikes and pink blush cheeks strolls past smiling, completely unaware. Warm golden prehistoric sunlight pours through the doorway across the hallway floorboards, contrasting with the cool indoor light. The hallway is real and photographic; everything beyond the door frame is handmade felt with visible wool fibers. 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, Kodak Portra colors, moment frozen.
