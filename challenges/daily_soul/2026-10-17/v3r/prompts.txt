A wide establishing shot of a cozy needle-felted mushroom village hidden deep in a lush forest, tall mossy trees, ferns and soft golden morning light streaming through the canopy. The houses are giant red, brown and cream spotted mushrooms with little round doors, glowing windows, tiny chimneys and flower boxes, connected by winding pebble paths, a little wooden bridge over a stream, a mushroom bakery and a mushroom post office. In the center foreground stand the villagers waving warmly at the viewer: Gomgom, a small round cream-colored needle-felted wool bear with soft fuzzy texture, small round ears with pale pink inner ears, round black bead eyes, a small dark brown nose, soft pink blush cheeks and a gentle closed-mouth smile, a small yellow felt star badge on its chest and a tiny brown felt satchel. Kong, a tiny round yellow needle-felted baby chick with a small orange beak and a tiny green sprout with two round leaves on its head, only one third the width of Gomgom's head, standing on top of Gomgom's head. Grandpa Owl, a round grey-brown felt owl with fluffy white eyebrows, small round spectacles and a mustard knitted cardigan, slightly taller than Gomgom. Friends: a felt rabbit with a mint scarf, a felt squirrel and a felt hedgehog, each about as tall as Gomgom. The other villagers go about their morning, the rabbit watering flowers, the squirrel carrying acorns, the hedgehog sweeping a doorstep, Grandpa Owl reading on a mushroom bench.
