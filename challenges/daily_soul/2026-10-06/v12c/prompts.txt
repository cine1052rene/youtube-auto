Blockbuster action movie poster, photorealistic, centered symmetrical composition exactly like a classic thriller one-sheet. A stern elderly man in his 70s with East Asian features, thick swept-back white hair, short white beard, dark thick-rimmed glasses, wearing a dark teal short-sleeved work shirt, stands behind a long teal-covered ironing board that runs horizontally across the lower third of the frame. A crumpled white dress shirt lies spread flat on the board in front of him. His eyes look straight down through his glasses at the white shirt with fierce concentration. His right hand grips a heavy old-fashioned cast-iron flat iron with a normal flat soleplate, raised at a steep angle just above the shirt as if about to strike; his left hand is pressed flat on the shirt smoothing the fabric. Steam rises from the shirt. A warm burnt-orange glow radiates behind his head and shoulders, fading into a deep dark teal background. Behind him, faint but clearly magical: a large thin-lined golden arcane magic circle, semi-transparent, never brighter than the man: concentric rings with a fine ornamental border band, an inner hexagram and seven-pointed star polygon, small satellite circles set into the rim holding tiny moon, sun, star and ringed-planet emblems, fine radial tick marks. In the top left and top right corners, two small symmetrical golden ornaments built only from graphic symbols: zodiac constellation line drawings with dots, astrological planet glyphs, and a hexagonal benzene-ring chemical structure inside small circles, arranged like seals of a magic circle, no letters, no words. At the bottom, bold heavy sans-serif poster typography in warm peach color: huge title "CRASE" and directly under it a small widely spaced tagline "ZERO WRINKLES", spelled exactly. These are the only readable words. No credits block, no small print, no logos, no text on clothing.
