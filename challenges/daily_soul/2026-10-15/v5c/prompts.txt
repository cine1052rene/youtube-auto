Hand-drawn 2D animated film still, warm golden candlelight. Low angle from the bottom of a grand marble staircase in a palace ballroom at midnight. A young nobleman in a navy coat with gold buttons stands on the stairs holding up one crystal high heel shoe, looking baffled and helpless, scratching his head.
